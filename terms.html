Terms of use

Effective November 14, 2026

Obsidian GSync Test is an owner-operated personal synchronization setup, using the GSync community plugin and the owner's Google Drive. It is not a public hosted service, does not offer customer accounts or subscriptions, and makes no service-level commitment.

Authorized use

Use this setup only with files, devices and Google accounts that you own or are authorized to access. The owner is responsible for choosing the synchronization folder, protecting device-local credentials, and granting only the access required for the intended use.

Synchronization behavior

Synchronization can create, modify and move files to trash. Conflicting edits may produce additional copies that need manual review. Connectivity, mobile background restrictions, authorization expiry, storage limits and software changes may delay or interrupt synchronization. Keep independent backups of important data; synchronized copies alone are not a backup history.
